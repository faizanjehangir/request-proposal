Air freight RFP · Component map

Follow the shipper request through routing, Pricing, approval, and customer acceptance.

Proposal lifecycle

  1. Intake and extraction

    The shipper provides a lane table and email. Intake imports the transit table and AI maps requirements to lanes with source excerpts.

    Passes forwardValidated lanes and requested services

  2. Routing and review

    The route engine checks directed flights, transit time, and services across all legs. The response team reviews gaps and no-offer lanes.

    Passes forwardSelected routes and offered services

  3. Pricing correspondence

    The email adapter requests rates from Pricing or the mock responder. AI extracts candidate rates and application rules check the scope.

    Passes forwardValidated prices per ton

  4. Quote and approval

    The workspace drafts a customer email with each lane’s route, service coverage, and price or no-offer reason. The response team approves the exact version.

    Passes forwardSent quote version and email thread

  5. Acceptance and kickoff

    Acceptance rules match the customer’s reply to the approved terms. The app writes the accepted proposal to SQL and queues a calendar invite.

    ProducesAccepted terms and kickoff status

Returns to the response team · Unclear requirements, routing or service exceptions, missing rates, and customer replies that change the terms.

Shared components

  • Workflow coordinator

    Applies review decisions, validates state transitions, and queues provider actions.

  • SQL and job queue

    Stores source evidence, quote versions, accepted proposals, and retryable jobs.

  • Email and calendar adapters

    Handle inbound replies, outbound requests and quotes, and kickoff booking.

  • Agent orchestration panel

    Shows inputs, routing results, exceptions, approvals, and delivery or retry status.
